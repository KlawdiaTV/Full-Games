Resident Evil 2 Remake — Leon
Really Enjoyed It

I really enjoyed playing Resident Evil 2 Remake, especially Leon's story. The gameplay was fun, the combat was satisfying, and I loved the atmosphere and the tension throughout the game. One thing that sometimes frustrated me was having to backtrack across the entire map just to find an item I needed somewhere else. There were moments when I felt like I was spending more time running around than actually making progress, especially when I couldn't remember where things were. But honestly, that didn't stop me from having a great time. I enjoyed the exploration, the puzzles, the fights, and the story. Even when the game made me run back and forth or stressed me out, I was still having fun. My only real complaint? I wish it had been longer! I would have happily spent more time playing because I genuinely enjoyed every moment of it. Final impression: Really Enjoyed It. A great survival horror experience with enjoyable gameplay, satisfying fights, and a story that kept me interested until the end. I just wanted more of it!

Resident Evil 2 Remake — Claire
Really Enjoyed It

Playing Claire's story after Leon's was a really interesting experience. I enjoyed seeing a different side of the story, but I definitely found this playthrough more challenging. What frustrated me the most was Mr. X showing up before I even had the chance to properly explore the police station. I like taking my time, looking around, collecting items, and figuring out where everything is, but apparently Mr. X had other plans! The puzzles were pretty much the same as in Leon's story, but solving them felt much harder when I constantly had someone chasing me. Having to travel back and forth across the map to find items or solve puzzles was already frustrating enough, and Mr. X definitely didn't make things any easier. Despite all that, I had an amazing time. I enjoyed the gameplay, the fights, and experiencing Claire's side of the story. Even with all the running around and the constant pressure, it was still a really enjoyable experience. Final impression: Really Enjoyed It. A more stressful experience than Leon's campaign, mostly thanks to Mr. X, but still an amazing game that I enjoyed from beginning to end!

The Lightkeeper (Live Version)
Interesting

The Lightkeeper was a terrifying experience, and I'm pretty sure this game gave me some new traumas. Seagulls and crows. Yes, SEAGULLS AND CROWS! I never thought I'd be scared of birds, but here we are. The atmosphere was creepy, the tension was constant, and there were plenty of moments when the game genuinely scared me. But being scared wasn't my only problem. There were several moments when I got completely stuck, wandering around trying to figure out what I was supposed to do next. The frustration was real, and I'm not sure I would have managed to finish the game without some help from my chat. Despite all the confusion, frustration, and unexpected bird-related trauma, I had a memorable experience. Finishing the game felt like an achievement, especially after everything it put me through. Final impression: A Terrifying Experience. A creepy, frustrating, and memorable horror game that tested both my nerves and my patience. And thanks to this game, I'll probably never look at seagulls and crows the same way again!
